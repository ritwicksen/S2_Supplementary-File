This MATLAB script models H⁺ diffusion in a clinopyroxene grain within a non-isothermal, finite-grain system.
User inputs: initial clinopyroxene H₂O content (C₀); final clinopyroxene H₂O content (C_f); grain size; activation energy (E_a); and pre-exponential factor (D₀).
The script plots measured H₂O concentrations along core-to-rim transects, and generates diffusion envelopes at user-specified time steps by varying C₀ and the SIMS half-length profile within its mean ± 1σ range.
Example H₂O concentrations and their corresponding distances from the crystal rim are provided in the spreadsheet Example_ConcentrationProfile.xlsx for overlaying onto the diffusion envelopes.
